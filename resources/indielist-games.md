# indielist.games — Indie Game Market Research Resource

[indielist.games](https://indielist.games/) maps indie game studios, publishers, and games into one graph for market research.

Useful for:

- Discovering indie game studios and publishers
- Reviewing published indie game catalogs
- Comparing sales estimates and price history
- Tracking submission status across 5,000+ published titles

Live resource: https://indielist.games/
